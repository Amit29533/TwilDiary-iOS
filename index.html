TwilDiary

Your watch diary, on your iPhone.

TwilDiary keeps track of the shows, movies and anime you watch: what you’re in the middle of, what’s next, your ratings, notes and lists. It works with the trackers you already use and keeps everything else on your phone.

Syncs with your trackers. Trakt, Simkl, MDBList and WeTrakr, signed in with your own accounts. Your edits go to the services you choose.

Backs up to your own Google Drive. An optional copy of your diary in a private app folder of your Drive, so a new phone gets everything back.

No ads, no tracking. TwilDiary doesn’t collect analytics or sell anything about you.

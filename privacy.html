TwilDiary

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

TwilDiary is an iPhone app for keeping a diary of what you watch. This policy explains what it does with your information. In short: your diary stays on your phone and in the services you connect yourself, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or tracked.

What TwilDiary keeps

  • Your diary: the titles you add, what you’ve watched and when, ratings, lists, notes and tags. It is stored on your iPhone.
  • Sign-ins and keys: the tokens for services you connect and any API keys you add. They are stored in your iPhone’s Keychain and sent only to the service they belong to.

TwilDiary has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Services you connect

When you sign in to a service, TwilDiary exchanges your diary with it directly from your phone, under that service’s own privacy policy:

  • Trakt, Simkl, MDBList and WeTrakr: to read your watch history, ratings and lists and to send the changes you make.
  • TMDB and AniList: to show posters, details, cast and episodes. Only the titles being looked up are sent.
  • AI providers you add a key for (Google Gemini, Groq, Requesty): only when you ask CineMind or use AI search, the question and the title it’s about are sent.
  • Jellyfin: if you connect your own server, to find which titles you have there.
  • A TwilDiary server you host yourself: optional; if you sign in to one, your diary is kept there too.

Google Drive

If you turn on Google Drive backup, TwilDiary asks for one permission: its own app data folder in your Drive (drive.appdata). This is a hidden folder only TwilDiary can use. TwilDiary cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

It uses that folder only to save and restore a backup of your diary. The backup is read and written by the app on your phone; it isn’t sent to anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

TwilDiary’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To stop it, turn off Google Drive in TwilDiary, or remove TwilDiary at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup folder can be deleted from Google Drive’s settings under “Manage apps”.

Deleting your information

Deleting TwilDiary removes the diary it keeps on your phone. Sign-ins saved in the iPhone’s Keychain can outlast the app (so a reinstall can reconnect); signing out of each service in TwilDiary first removes them. Data in services you connected stays in those services until you remove it there.

Children

TwilDiary isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions can be raised on GitHub.

# Stats dashboard based on the full-scroll reference

Stats at `/preview/activity` now uses `StatsDashboard.svelte` and the pure `domain/stats.ts` calculations. It retains the existing app header, bottom navigation, live diary controller, API/authentication boundaries, and the separate Recently watched page.

## Sections

- Watch-date coverage shows dated logs, undated logs, missing runtimes and future-dated entries. Review dates opens Recently watched filtered to unknown dates; it does not invent dates or claim to edit existing watch records.
- The original quick row stays **7D, 30D, 90D, 1Y, All**. Overlapping controls share one canonical state: 7D ↔ This Week (Monday through today), 30D ↔ This Month (month to date), and 1Y ↔ This Year or the chosen historic year. The dropdown retains its existing calendar choices; no Today/Week/Month buttons were added to the row. 90D remains a rolling range, and All includes every usable dated watch. Old saved 7D/30D/1Y states migrate to the matching calendar period; an old selected bar is cleared if its dates have changed. Both control handlers reset selected details and horizontal chart position on a new choice.
- Watch-time totals count each movie/episode and rewatch. Average divides the runtime estimate by the selected calendar-day span. Comparisons align corresponding weekdays or calendar dates in the preceding week/month/year, clamping leap-day and short-month boundaries. The label states the comparison being made. 90D compares the preceding 90 days; All omits comparison. Day views retain daily bars through 31 days; Year groups into calendar months; longer rolling/All views aggregate into at most 30 bars. The busiest day is calculated from positive known daily runtime, never invented from unknown runtime.
- Streaks use distinct local watch days. Current streak includes a run ending yesterday until today has elapsed. Longest streak uses all valid dated logs. The heatmap shows 12 Monday–Sunday weeks; future days in the current week are disabled and excluded from the active-day denominator. Clicking a day opens date-filtered history.
- Backlog shows status counts, planned runtime, the shortest known remaining runtimes first, and watching titles last watched at least 30 days ago. Titles with undated logs are excluded from stalled suggestions because their most recent actual watch date cannot be established. Only aired regular episodes enter episode progress; specials and future episodes do not. Future-dated watches cannot manufacture progress. Episode watch IDs are scoped to their title. Incomplete catalogues, invalid air dates or missing runtimes show Time unknown instead of guessing; remote series without a season manifest are treated as incomplete. Drop calls the existing undoable status command; no automatic status changes run when Stats loads.
- Genre mix shows watched-title coverage, not pie-slice contribution percentages. Each title counts once per genre. The top five are visible initially; All other genres expands the list. The Other summary counts distinct titles in those remaining genres rather than adding overlapping genre counts.

## Date and data rules

UTC surrogate dates represent local calendar days in the viewer's timezone so DST, leap days, week/year boundaries and local midnight behave consistently. Invalid/unknown dates are excluded from period charts and streaks. Future local days are excluded. Revoked events, missing titles and deleted titles are excluded. Missing runtimes still count as watches but contribute no invented minutes. Planned-series runtime requires sufficient season/episode metadata.

Watch-date validation uses the same strict instant validator as watch logging, rejecting impossible calendar dates that JavaScript would otherwise normalize into another month. Review dates and Recently watched display/filter those legacy invalid dates as unknown. None of this rewrites stored history.

The page starts on Today and remembers its period, year, selected bar and expansion limits. Home’s Stats shortcut explicitly uses `period=today`, overriding a previously selected period on entry; back/forward still restores the user's selection. Horizontal chart, streak summary and title rails participate in existing view memory. History accepts `unknown=1` and `from`/`to` deep links so all dashboard links are functional. The app does not place API secrets or private server code in the UI.

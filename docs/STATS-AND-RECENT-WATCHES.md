# Stats and Recently watched

Stats remains the existing main tab at `/preview/activity`. Its watch-time periods, summary cards and selected movies/shows use horizontal rails. Year selection resets the chart to January; period selection resets its rail to the start. The period and year selectors share a nonwrapping top row, with compact widths on phones so the period selector moves left when the year picker appears.

The totals keep their original meaning but use plain labels and explanations: Total watches counts each logged movie or episode including rewatches; Movies & shows counts distinct watched titles; With watch dates counts logs with a known watch date. Watch Time and the genre chart use the same vocabulary.

The genre legend starts with the five largest genres. Selecting another chart segment reveals that genre beneath the five. Show all genres expands the full legend, and Show top 5 collapses it. Hiding legend rows never changes the chart's percentages, genre rankings or watch counts.

Watch history moved to `/preview/recently-watched`, opened by Home's Recently watched → All history link. This is a separate drill-down page, not an extra bottom-navigation item. Its top back arrow is hidden at the user's request; native browser/swipe Back still returns to Home or the prior page. A title opened from the history returns to the same history page with its filters, loaded rows and scroll position remembered.

The history page supports title search, From/To date filters, Today, Last 7 days, This month, and unknown dates. The separate On date field was removed at the user's request; a single day can still be selected with equal From/To values. A filter icon beside Your watch history toggles the date panel and remembers its open state. Date shortcuts use the shared chip theme, including dark text on the active accent background and active-state feedback for every preset. Date presets use local calendar days, including across year boundaries. Unknown-only mode clears and disables date bounds so it cannot silently conflict with a date range. The date inputs use shrinkable grid columns and explicit width constraints to avoid the phone overflow shown in the report.

Watch Time also offers This Week: Monday through Sunday in the viewer's timezone, with seven daily bars and the same selectable movie/show details. Week boundaries use calendar-day arithmetic so a week can cross months, years, or daylight-saving changes without losing or double-counting days. Existing rewatches, unknown-date/runtime handling and the year-picker alignment remain unchanged. Added regression tests cover local midnight boundaries, a week spanning New Year, Monday rollover and repeated DST hours.

Validation: 279 unit/database/runtime checks, 3 compiled PWA tests, a production build, and zero Svelte/TypeScript errors or warnings. After the final top-row alignment change, type checks, production build and compiled PWA tests passed again. Regression coverage includes smaller-genre selection/expansion, date ranges across year/leap-day boundaries, exact-date search through both ends of the local day, and Home → history → title → Back navigation. No owner watch data or database schema was changed. Phone appearance still needs a physical-device check; no browser visual verification was available in this session.

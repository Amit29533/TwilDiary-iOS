### Design Lab (iPhone app)

**Settings → Design Lab** (under Look and layout) keeps the few redrawn pieces the owner wanted as choices, in every theme (owner's request, November 2026). Its starting points are **Original** (none of them on, the default), **Studio** (all of them) and **Custom**. The choices left: Home (title left with the sparkle on the right, episode capsules on the hero, grouped Recently watched, Recommended's match on the poster, in the caption or off), Glance (episode capsules on Last watched, compact grouped rows), Title page (parental guide coloured by severity) and People (the person's own photo blurred behind the header, the name in white). **Feel** sets where rating badges sit (as is, top, bottom, off), corners (squarer, standard, rounder) and haptics. Search, Explore, and Calendar and history are listed as coming. Code: `ios/App/Design/Studio.swift` (`Studio.on(_:)`, `StudioLab`). Studio was briefly a theme; `DesignLabMigration` moves anyone on it to Native with the Studio preset.

Everything else from those redraws is now simply how the app looks (owner's decision: "make it default, no setting needed"): a firm press with no bounce, grey layout switches, plain hero and Last watched labels with a darker fade, the This week bar, tight poster captions, the date on the episode line, tinted + buttons, dimmer filters with nothing in them (Glance, Library status, Search kinds), Caught up on Glance; on title pages the age rating on the year line, grey genres, the scores in one card, a tinted Log a watch, the quiet status card and initials for companies without a logo; on people pages the facts together, a quiet From card, the biography first, one or two watched titles as rows, a name tile for missing posters, count pills, lead roles first in Known for and released titles first in Shows and Movies (done app-side in `StudioPeople`, because the shared ranking in `PersonSections` matches the web app and its golden tests). See-all pages keep the picture across the top, then the title on its own (no symbol) with a line under it ("Pedro Pascal · 24 titles"). Library opens with two rows of posters and a See all button, then the genres in one card. Statistics has no dates bar, blue only in charts and the current month, a grey chosen range, Hall of fame and the watch-time split as grouped cards, and small Movies/Shows and Cast/Voice switches. The Episodes pages show up to 16 episodes at once (a longer season folds the rest into groups marked with a stack icon, not a tick), use one grey All · Watched · To watch control in every theme (the title page's Episodes section has the same control with All · To watch, beside the grey list/grid switch), keep the season's percent small and grey, give grid cards two lines for the name and one details line, wait for stills on a plain grey tile (the episode number when there is none) and call every unwatched episode "Unwatched". Motion that came with the redraws: a firm press with no bounce, the episode track's capsule filling and its outline moving on, the Glance + button's bar, rolling count, star burst and success tap landing together, rows moving into Caught up instead of jumping, a list/grid switch whose thumb slides (with a light tap) while the content fades into the new layout instead of morphing (`layoutFade` in `Motion.swift`), the title page's numbers and Log a watch / Rewatch label changing in place, and This week's day bars rising when you log something rather than growing in every time Home opens. Reduce Motion turns all of it off. Nothing grows as it loads (owner's request: a shelf that expands pushes the page and drops frames): loading shapes are the size of what replaces them (`DiaryLoadingPlaceholder`'s poster, standard and Home shelves use the real posters' widths and caption fonts), Search keeps room for the trending row and its tags, the list count on Official Lists and Featured Franchises, and two lines each for a person's name and Known for, and the title page's Awards keeps three rows' room until its ceremonies are read. Removed: the shorter Glance header, page dots on the hero and spelled-out award counts. A watch dated 1 January 1970 (an import's placeholder) counts as undated in Statistics, Home and History (`DiaryDate.watched`).

All six themes share the floating capsule navigation layout and a whole-tab selection pill, with matching safe-area clearance for content and Undo messages. Each theme retains its own accent and material treatment. Navigation, filters, progress, primary actions, personal ratings and accent-based charts share theme tokens. Semantic success/error colors, gold community ratings, artwork and genre category colors keep their meaning.

The device-local preference uses only `twildiary.theme.v1`. It does not modify diary data or sync settings. Other tabs on the same origin follow changes. A small allowlisted pre-paint script restores the theme before rendering; the PWA caches this public script for offline startup. Invalid preferences fall back to Twilight. If saving is blocked, the picker reports that the choice applies to the current session.

Liquid Glass uses a pure black AMOLED canvas, dark content surfaces, a floating capsule navigation bar and silver highlights on translucent controls. It takes material inspiration from Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines for [materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials). It keeps artwork sharp and the shared wordmark header opaque. It adds no page-root transforms, navigation delays, motion or extra status-bar spacing. It respects reduced transparency with opaque controls and honors increased contrast. The previously deferred iOS Home Screen header rendering issue remains separate.

## Validation

- Unit tests cover all theme IDs, corrupted/denied storage, dedicated-key writes, the actual pre-paint script and normal-text contrast for accents and filled buttons.
- Production PWA tests check that the startup script is cached and served offline.
- Browser checks at 393 px and 320 px verify the picker fits without horizontal overflow. All six themes were selected, their applied accent and selected state checked, and Liquid Glass was retained after a reload.
- Mobile Glass settings and Home screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/themes-glass-phone.png` and `docs/screenshots/themes-glass-home-phone.png`.

Native iPhone rendering still needs device verification; desktop browser checks cannot establish native WebKit blur behavior.

### Settings layout (iPhone app)

The Profile tab's settings are grouped as **Look and layout** (Theme, Design Lab, Layout — Home, Explore and tabs —, Posters), **Library and tracking** (Public ratings, Hide spoilers, Files and drives, JellySpace, Trash), **Shortcuts** (Watch history, Airing calendar), **Sync and data** (Connections, Sync and backup, Export, Import) and **General** (Notifications, Opens on, Language and region, Time zone, Reduce motion). The Theme page keeps the themes and Native's light or dark choice; Haptics is in Design Lab → Feel.

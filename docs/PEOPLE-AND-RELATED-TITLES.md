# People, related titles and small artwork

Saved and catalog title pages include a horizontally scrollable “You might also like” row using TMDB's title-specific recommendations. Posters open the saved library title when present, otherwise the existing catalog detail page. The current title, duplicates and adult results are excluded. The row uses the standard compact 2:3 browsing covers; Glance poster sizes are unchanged.

Every cast portrait links to `/preview/person/:id`. The person page includes a portrait header, the role from the originating title, expandable biography, Facts & trivia directly above On your server, Known for, and separate Shows and Movies shelves. Each shelf opens the same title detail pages. The complete provider filmography is available through Show more; only the first 24 covers per row render initially.

On your server includes only matching TMDB identities with a currently confirmed `present` disk location. A title merely being Planned or in the library does not count as a disk copy. Movies and series with the same numeric provider ID remain separate. Known for is ranked from provider popularity with an episode-count weight to reduce one-off TV appearances; it is not an official curated TMDB known-for list. Trivia uses provider facts, aliases, counts and the earliest listed movie. A show's first-air date is never treated as the person's debut year. No invented anecdotes are added.

All provider requests stay behind owner authentication. Public recommendations cache for one day in D1; person biographies/credits cache for seven days. Both can use their last valid data during a provider outage. Session memory caches avoid repeat client requests; current library membership is derived from the live workspace rather than persisted in public metadata. Schema migrations 0010 and 0011 add cache tables without changing library/watch data.

Episode thumbnails are now 96×64px (80×56px on narrow phones), aligned to the top of the title in normal and expanded rows. Expanded synopsis remains tap-to-reveal. Recently watched and History use unobscured 60×90px portrait covers (56×84px on narrow phones), with 6px padding for a compact border. Search and existing list cards use 72×108px covers (64×96px on narrow phones). Tint layers stay behind the cover and text. Small list-card badges move beside the text; grid badges stay in place. Search uses higher-resolution poster sources for phone displays.

Glance has All, Movies, Shows and Anime filters in a horizontal strip. Movies includes regular and anime movies; Anime includes anime shows and movies. All remains the current Watching list. Film cards use their existing Log watch action instead of season/episode controls. The computer button filters to titles with a confirmed present disk copy. Disposable UI checks verified both film categories appear under Movies and logging a movie updates its card and offers Undo without writing owner data.

Watch status now saves atomically with history changes. A new movie viewing marks Completed. New show episode watches move the title to Watching, or Completed once every regular episode across every known regular season is watched. Expected episode counts must be known and the corresponding episode records present; unloaded/unknown seasons prevent premature completion. Unwatched specials do not block completion, and repeat viewings count once. Removing a completion-defining watch returns Completed to Watching, and Undo restores completion when appropriate. A movie remains Completed while any older active viewing remains. Operation retries do not reapply status over later manual edits.
